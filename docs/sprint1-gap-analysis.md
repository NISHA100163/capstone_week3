# Sprint 1 – Gap Analysis

## Current MVP

The current Product Catalogue provides basic product information and supports the main catalogue requirements.

Based on the industry investigation of Bunnings, Kmart and JB Hi-Fi, several gaps were identified between our current Product Catalogue and real-world retail systems.

---

## Identified Gaps

### 1. Product Availability

Real systems provide detailed product availability information such as online stock, in-store availability, Click & Collect and delivery availability.

Our Product Catalogue currently has limited availability information.

### 2. Product Variants

Products may have different variants such as:

- Size
- Colour
- Storage capacity
- Model
- Product type

Our system should allow optional variant data depending on the product category.

### 3. Promotional Pricing

JB Hi-Fi showed that products may include:

- Regular price
- Sale price
- Discount amount
- Promotion status

Our Product Catalogue currently focuses mainly on a single product price.

### 4. Ratings and Reviews

Bunnings, Kmart and JB Hi-Fi display customer ratings and review counts.

Our Product Catalogue does not currently provide customer rating or review information.

### 5. Related Products

Bunnings and JB Hi-Fi display related or similar products.

Our Product Catalogue does not currently provide product recommendations.

### 6. Wishlist

Kmart provides wishlist functionality that allows customers to save products for later.

Our current Product Catalogue does not include a wishlist feature.

### 7. Stock Notifications

Kmart allows customers to register for notifications when sold-out products become available.

Our Product Catalogue does not currently provide stock notification functionality.

### 8. Product-Specific Specifications

Different product categories require different information.

For example:

- Furniture may require size, colour and collection.
- Electronics may require model number, storage and technical specifications.
- Tools may require technical features and kit contents.

Our Product Catalogue should allow flexible category-specific specifications.

---

# Improvement Priorities

## Must Have

The following features are essential for the Product Catalogue:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Brand
- Category
- SKU
- Product Type
- Price
- Description
- Product Image
- Availability Status

## Should Have

The following features would improve the Product Catalogue:

- Product variants
- Model number
- Size
- Colour
- Product specifications
- Sale price
- Discount information
- Delivery availability
- Click & Collect availability
- In-store availability

## Nice To Have

The following features could be added in future development:

- Customer ratings
- Customer reviews
- Wishlist
- Stock notifications
- Related products
- Similar products
- Product visualisation
- Product recommendations

---

# Gap Analysis Findings

The comparison with Bunnings, Kmart and JB Hi-Fi showed that our Product Catalogue already contains the basic structure required to display products, but several features could improve the system.

The highest priority is to ensure that core product data is complete and accurate. Availability information, product variants and category-specific specifications should also be considered important improvements.

More advanced features such as customer ratings, reviews, wishlist, stock notifications and product recommendations can be added in future development after the core Product Catalogue is stable.
